Guides
Before you print
  1. Set scale to 100% / Actual size, not "Fit to page".
  2. Measure the 1 inch bar at the bottom of a sheet to confirm.
  3. Cut out a circle, line its edge up with the ornament, slide carbon paper underneath, tape down and trace.
