    Dotsville Stock Exchange

    Residents with spare savings trade shares every evening. Prices drift toward what a company is worth, about three years of profit plus its cash, and get pushed around by confidence, momentum, interest rates and the balance of buyers and sellers. Businesses go public when they need money to grow, most often when the market is hot.
